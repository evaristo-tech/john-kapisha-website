My story

My name is John Kapisha, and my journey has taken me from Zambia to Dartmouth College in Hanover, New Hampshire.

I have always believed in the importance of education, perseverance, and making the most of opportunities. My experiences have shaped my interest in understanding how economies function, how resources are allocated, and how education and economic opportunities can improve people's lives.

At Dartmouth, I am pursuing Economics with an intended minor in Environmental Science. My academic interests continue to develop through exposure to investment management, energy systems, business problem-solving, and research.

Outside the classroom, I have gained experience in different professional environments, from agricultural project work in Zambia to student programming and library operations at Dartmouth. These experiences have taught me the importance of adaptability, communication, collaboration, and learning from people with different backgrounds.

Being selected as a King Scholar has been an important part of my educational journey. It has also strengthened my commitment to leadership, service, and finding meaningful ways to contribute to society.
